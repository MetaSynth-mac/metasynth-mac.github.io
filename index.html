MetaSynth for Mac

MetaSynth is a revolutionary electronic music and sound design environment for macOS. Six distinct rooms offer unique ways to sculpt sound—from granular synthesis to spectral sequencing. Not a soft synth, but a complete studio for composition, mixing, and unprecedented sound creation. Version 1.5 introduces Live Looping in Image Synth and ArtMatic transport control.

Effects Room

Envelope-controllable DSP effects, including granular synthesis and FFT processing. Transform audio with precision and depth.

Image Synth

Paint sound directly: color maps to spatial position, brightness to amplitude. Enhanced architecture with Live Looping for intuitive, visual synthesis in any tuning.

Image Filter Room

Dynamic stereo filters via an Image Synth interface. Craft extraordinary effects that stand alone as a creative powerhouse.

Spectrum Synth

Spectral granular sequencing: analyze sounds into editable events. Reshape spectra in ways unimaginable elsewhere.

Sequencer and Montage Rooms

Lightweight non-MIDI composition for melodies and loops. 24-track mixing and recording for full production workflows.

How It Works

Step 1: Explore the Rooms

Select from six specialized rooms, each a standalone tool for sound design, synthesis, or sequencing.

Step 2: Sculpt and Paint

Use visual interfaces like Image Synth to draw sounds, apply filters, or sequence spectra with granular control.

Step 3: Compose and Mix

Build phrases in Sequencer Room, then refine in Montage Room's 24-track environment.

Step 4: Export and Integrate

Render your creations or export for use in other DAWs as your secret weapon.
